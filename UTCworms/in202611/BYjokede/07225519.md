## Agreed so far

- **Path:** `UTCworms/inYYYYMM/<by|BY>AUTHOR/DDHHMMSS.EXT`. All times UTC.
  `UTCworms`, `in` and `by` are literal.
- **Prefix case:** `BY` if AUTHOR starts with a lowercase letter, otherwise
  `by` (digits and punctuation are full-height, so `by` contrasts better).
- **AUTHOR:** a Claude session's six-character branch nonce (this one is
  `jokede`), or a human username. Each conversation is a distinct author, so
  authors don't collide.
- **Timestamps:** they only need to be unique and to increase over time. On a
  same-second clash, bump the seconds.
- **Extensions:** keep to 8.3 unless the toolchain demands a longer one
  (`.java` is compelling enough).
- **Write-once:** worms are never edited or deleted. Root files may be updated.
- **Branch:** commit straight to `main`, worms and `.md` files alike. No
  session branches: the structure exists to make branching unnecessary.
- **Recording:** all code written in a conversation goes into the wormstream
  (only there, no duplicates elsewhere), plus the transcript as who said what
  when, in pieces, each covering everything since the last piece.
- **Redaction:** transcripts leave out the secret, the sensitive and the
  legally actionable. The repo is public. Details to be settled.
- **Docs:** `README.md` covers the content and points to `WORMS.md` (the
  structure) and `CLAUDE.md` (rules for sessions, stated forcefully).
