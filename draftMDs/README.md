# draftMDs: drafts that stay drafts for now

Drafts, MD or TXT, where breaking changes are expected. Refer to
[`../STYLE.md`](../STYLE.md) for how drafts are handled. A draft is better
retired by amending it, in place, to say it is inoperative, than deleted.

1. Refer to [`PARADIGM.md`](PARADIGM.md) first: the governing principle,
   "nothing is hidden", in brief, with pointers to the drafts below.
   Intended for the root.
2. Refer to [`TIERS.md`](TIERS.md) for which text wins, and what may change
   where.
3. Refer to [`ROLES.md`](ROLES.md) for owners, drones and bees.
4. Refer to [`COMMITS.md`](COMMITS.md) for what commit messages should
   contain, and for flags.
5. Refer to [`BUNDLES.md`](BUNDLES.md) for how bundles are dropped and
   closed.
6. Refer to [`SECRETS.md`](SECRETS.md) for what must never be committed.
7. Refer to [`BUZZ.md`](BUZZ.md) for the working picture of how AnCl3 links to
   the Buzz relay (issue #2).
